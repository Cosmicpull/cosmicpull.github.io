What CosmicPull is

CosmicPull reads two birth charts side by side — a Korean Four Pillars (Saju) chart and a Western birth chart — and describes what traditional rules say about the pattern between them. It is a reflective, symbolic experience. It does not diagnose, predict events, or give medical, legal, or financial advice.

There is no account

You do not sign up, and CosmicPull never asks for your name, email address, phone number, or address. Nothing in the app requires you to identify yourself.

What stays on your device

These are stored only on your phone and are never sent anywhere:

  • your birth date, birth time, and birth place, and those of anyone you add
  • your saved bonds and the readings created from them
  • your daily check-ins and your private reflection notes
  • your notification preference

Deleting the app removes them. You can also remove them from inside the app at any time — see Your controls below.

What leaves your device

Usage events. So that we can tell which parts of the app are worth building, CosmicPull records that an action happened: a reading was created, a Bond Lens was opened, a share sheet was opened. Each event name and each of its values comes from a fixed list defined in the code. No free-form text is ever attached, so nothing you write or enter can travel with an event. A random identifier, generated on your device and never derived from your birth details, tells one device apart from another. These events are processed by PostHog.

Coming back. One of those events records that the app was opened on a day it had not been opened before. It carries how long you have had the app — as a range such as "day 1", "day 2" or "more than a month" — and never a date or a time. Your device keeps the two dates the range is worked out from and does not send them, so we can see that people return without holding a record of when any one person was here. Deleting your on-device data clears those two dates, after which the app counts you as new.

Fan birth-time polls. If you vote in a poll about a public figure's birth time, your choice is sent to our server. It travels with a one-way hash of an identifier stored on your device, not with the identifier itself, and with a one-way hash of a coarse network address used only to limit abuse. Voting again replaces your earlier vote rather than adding one. After 14 months your vote is deleted, which also means it stops counting towards the result and you are free to vote again. A poll result is what fans believe and is never presented as verified fact.

Purchases. If you buy a membership, the payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google. CosmicPull never sees your card, your name, or your store account. So that the app knows what you bought, an anonymous identifier is created for that install, and the record kept under it holds the plan you bought and when it renews — nothing else. No birth details are part of it. Purchase events reach us through RevenueCat.

Written readings. Some readings are written in sentences by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Nothing is sent for them until you allow it: the app asks the first time you press a button that writes one, and you can turn it off again under About & Privacy. When you ask for one, the calculated placements it is written from — for example the characters of your Four Pillars, the signs of your planets and the years of your ten-year cycles — go through our server to Anthropic, which writes the text and sends it back. Your name (which CosmicPull never asks for) and your birth date, time and place are not sent, though the placements are calculated from them. Our server keeps no copy of what was sent or written; it keeps only counts used to cap how many readings can be asked for in a day (see How long things are kept). The written reading is stored on your device. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models and deletes it within 30 days, except where its systems flag a request under its usage policy, in which case it may keep it for up to two years.

Free readings. A free reader can open some readings in full — Self, two areas of their choice, the first month's reading and one follow-up question in each of those areas. So that deleting everything does not open them all over again, your device keeps one random code, generated on the phone and unrelated to you or your birth details, and our server records which free readings were used under a one-way hash of that code. The record holds only which readings were used and when: no birth details, no reading text, no account. This code is not removed by deleting everything — that is the one thing the delete leaves, and the app says so when you use it. On iPhone it also outlasts removing the app; on Android it does not.

What is never collected

  • your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • your precise location
  • your contacts, photos, or camera
  • advertising identifiers — CosmicPull contains no advertising and no cross-app tracking
  • the content of your reflection notes

CosmicPull does not sell personal information, and does not share it for advertising.

Photos

If you save a share card, the image is written to your photo library at that moment using the permission you grant. CosmicPull does not read your photo library.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled and delivered by your device. Their content is built on your phone. Turning them off in the app or in system settings stops them.

Ages 13 and up

CosmicPull is not intended for children under 13. A birth date under 13 cannot be saved as your own profile. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us at cosmicpull9@gmail.com and we will delete anything associated with that device.

Your controls

Inside the app, under About & Privacy, you can:

  • clear your saved birth profile
  • clear individual reflection dates or your whole reflection history
  • remove any saved bond
  • delete everything CosmicPull has stored on the device

Because there is no account, deleting on-device data is immediate and complete, with one exception, said in the app when you delete: the random code behind Free readings above stays, so free readings already used stay used. Usage events already recorded cannot be tied back to you by name; to have the records for a device removed, send us the request at cosmicpull9@gmail.com.

A membership is not on this device and is not deleted with it. It belongs to your store account, so Restore purchases brings it back after a delete, a reinstall, or a new phone.

How long things are kept

On-device data stays until you delete it or remove the app.

Poll votes are kept for 14 months and then deleted.

Usage events are not ours to delete on a schedule. They are held by PostHog and removed on PostHog's own clock — one year. That window is PostHog's to set: it cannot be made shorter, and we do not ask for it to be made longer.

Counters the server keeps only to limit abuse are shorter-lived: the daily narration allowance is deleted after 90 days and the vote rate-limit windows after 30, because neither means anything once its day has passed.

The free-readings record is kept for 24 months after a device's last free reading, and then deleted.

Purchase records are kept while the membership is active and afterwards as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the effective date above changes and the app will say so before the change takes effect.
