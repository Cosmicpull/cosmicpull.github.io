Support

Questions, problems, or a request about your data: write to cosmicpull9@gmail.com. We read every message.

Deleting your data

CosmicPull has no account. Your birth details, saved bonds and notes are stored on your device. To remove them, open the app and go to About & Privacy → Delete my data on this device. Deleting the app removes them as well.

One random code stays after that delete: it records which free readings have been used, so they stay used. It holds nothing about you or your birth details, and it is removed from our server 24 months after its last use.

Written readings

Nothing is sent to write a reading with AI until you allow it, and you can turn this off under About & Privacy. The privacy policy says exactly what is sent and to whom.

Membership

A membership belongs to your App Store or Google Play account. To cancel, use your store's subscription settings. After reinstalling or moving to a new phone, use Restore purchases in the app.
