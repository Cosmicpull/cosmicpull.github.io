What CosmicPull is

CosmicPull reads Korean Four Pillars (Saju) and Western astrology side by side and describes what traditional rules say about a chart, a day, and two charts together. Its readings are for reflection. They do not predict events, and they are not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice. Decisions about your health, money, work and relationships are yours to make, with people qualified to help where that matters.
